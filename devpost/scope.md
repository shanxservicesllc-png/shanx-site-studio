# ShanX Site Studio
An offline-capable editor that turns a small service business's details into a portable one-page website.

## The Unique Kernel
Edit, preview, and export a complete responsive HTML page that remains usable offline, without a hosting subscription or locked-in editing service.

## Who It's For
A small service-business owner who needs a straightforward page describing their work and how customers can contact them.

## The Core Loop
Enter business name/description, up to three services, email, phone and a call to action. Switch between two themes and see the preview update. Export the standalone HTML, open it offline, inspect the contact links, then edit and export again when details change.

## Inspiration & Identity
The owner's existing ShanX Site Studio idea, freshly implemented through the official curriculum. Practical and simple. The product name is ShanX Site Studio (spelled S H A N X); it makes no price guarantee or verified sale claim. Specific visual identities of the two themes will be established in PRD.

## Why This Matters to the Learner
Rebuild the same useful idea correctly, preserving genuine planning decisions and independent proof rather than retroactively claiming the earlier prototype followed the required process.

## What "Working" Looks Like
A short demonstration enters sample business details, changes theme, exports a real HTML file, opens it without internet, verifies email/telephone link targets, changes details and exports again. HTML/script-shaped input appears as inert text; it must not execute in preview or export. Clicking contact links may invoke external phone/email software; offline page availability does not mean offline call/email delivery.

## The POC Boundary
One service-business page; up to three services; two themes; live preview; editable details; repeatable responsive standalone HTML export; safe rendering. No old application source is reused. Run independent positive/negative tests and real browser/offline checks. Proof Before Done may supplement supported artifact checks, never certify the whole project.

## Later
Additional capabilities remain outside this PoC unless the owner requests and reviews a scope change. No added feature backlog is presumed.

## Explicitly Cut
- Hosting/publication automation: exported file is not a hosted website.
- Payments, billing, subscriptions or sales claims: unnecessary to prove the editor/export loop.
- Unnecessary external services/dependencies: preserve offline portability.
- Copying/forking/refactoring the earlier implementation: this is a new competition build.

## Reverse Loop Trace
Finished portable website -> accurate offline page and inert input -> editor, preview, export artifact -> approved PRD/spec and fresh build slices -> independent browser/link/security checks -> logs, hashes, receipts and genuine owner review.
